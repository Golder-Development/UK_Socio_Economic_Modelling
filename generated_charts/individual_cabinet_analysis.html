Key Insights

  • Tenure variation: Average cabinet member tenure is 3.3 years, but ranges from 0.0 to 16.5 years.
  • Appointment patterns: Most members (168 out of 311) serve in a single continuous or non-overlapping period. Those with 31 or more spells represent the sacrificial pawn pattern.
  • Spell consistency: Members with CV < 0.5 show consistent spell lengths (stalwarts), while those with CV > 1.5 show highly varied patterns (pawns).
  • Post rotation: 30 members held 4+ different posts, indicating career mobility within Cabinet.
